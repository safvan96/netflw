Coriolis mass flow meter designed for high-viscosity and demanding process applications. Directly measures mass flow rate, density, and temperature simultaneously through the Coriolis effect. Ideal for sugar massecuite, molasses, and other viscous fluids.

Technical Recommendation: Based on our evaluation of your process fluid (A Seed massecuite), we recommend Coriolis mass flow technology instead of electromagnetic for the DN200 line. Massecuite is a highly viscous sugar-process fluid; Coriolis meters provide accurate mass measurement regardless of fluid viscosity, density variations, and flow profile — which electromagnetic meters cannot guarantee in such conditions.
